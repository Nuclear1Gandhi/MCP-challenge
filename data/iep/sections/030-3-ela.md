Baseline (What can the student currently do?): 
Jasmine's current ELA grade is a 1.8. Riverstone Prep uses a standards-based grading system where 3 reflects 
full mastery of grade-level material and 2 reflects partial mastery. Currently, Jasmine is at a third-grade reading 
level, as measured by iReady in Fall 2025. Her subcategory scores are Vocabulary: Grade 3, Literature 
Comprehension: Grade 3, and Informational Text Comprehension: Grade 2. This makes it difficult for her to 
understand and write about the rigorous texts in ELA.  Jasmine demonstrates partial and inconsistent 
engagement in class tasks and does not consistently put forth the full effort seen in her best moments during 
writing assignments. This makes it challenging to collect accurate and reliable data on her independent skills in 
class. When independently engaged and attempting work with accommodations, she continues to experience 
difficulty accessing and comprehending grade level texts, e.g., often not annotating, struggling to use reading 
strategies, etc. She often turns in reading homework with comprehension questions that have answers ranging 
from 0% to 50% accuracy when completed independently.  Jasmine is new to Riverstone Prep and still learning 
our writing system. She is still working to create full paragraphs with claims, evidence introduced with context, 
and analysis that accurately reflects her reasoning for selecting her evidence. While effort is inconsistent, 
Jasmine is starting to adapt to our writing system and showing growth in finding the value of using graphic 
organizers and supports such as checklists. Her use of these supports is helping her find success and be more 
accurate with her responses. Jasmine requires significant adult support to break down complex vocabulary, 
sentence structures, and key ideas, as well as to organize her thinking for writing tasks. With 1:1 adult support, 
Jasmine can participate in writing activities at grade level, but her independent performance remains below 
grade-level expectations with 50% accuracy.
