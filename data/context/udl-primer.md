# UDL and differentiation quick primer

This file is author-written synthesis for MCP hosts—use it as optional pedagogy context alongside the lesson and IEP resources.

## Engagement

- Offer meaningful choices (task, tool, or pacing) when the objective stays fixed.
- Connect tasks to student interests or authentic contexts where possible.

## Representation

- Present key ideas in more than one modality (verbal, visual, symbolic).
- Pre-teach vocabulary and provide sentence stems tied to the lesson objective.

## Action and expression

- Allow varied ways to show understanding (oral, written, constructed response, simplified product).
- Keep success criteria visible and refer to them during independent work.

## Differentiation vs modification

- **Accommodations** change how a student accesses the same grade-level expectation.
- **Modifications** adjust the expectation or rigor—document when the IEP calls for them.

## Classroom reminders

- Preview routines before transitions; signal wait time after questions.
- Pair cognitive demand with a compensation named in the IEP (e.g., calculator, graphic organizer, chunked directions).
